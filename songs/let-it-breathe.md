# Let It Breathe — Suno Build Sheet (PC)

## Step 1 — Open Suno in Custom Mode
1. On your PC, go to suno.com → **Create**.
2. Switch the toggle to **Custom** (so you get separate Lyrics + Style boxes).
3. Title: **Let It Breathe**

## Step 2 — Paste into the "Style of Music" box

```
Soulful Americana, acoustic country-soul ballad, 72 BPM, D major, 6/8 sway, warm fingerpicked acoustic guitar, upright piano, soft brushed drums, round bass, pedal steel swells, Hammond organ pad, raspy weathered male vocals, half-spoken intimate verses, belted gospel-tinged chorus, choir harmonies on hook, room reverb, vinyl warmth, building from lonely to hopeful, cathartic, intimate, cinematic final chorus
```

Short version (if your plan caps Style at 200 characters):

```
Soulful Americana ballad, 72 BPM, fingerpicked acoustic, piano, pedal steel, brushed drums, raspy male vocals, spoken verses, gospel choir chorus, lonely to hopeful
```

## Step 3 — Advanced Options
| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Exclude Styles** | `EDM, trap hi-hats, autotune, female lead vocal, heavy distortion, dubstep` | Keeps it organic and keeps one male lead |
| **Vocal Gender** | Male | Matches the first-person story |
| **Weirdness** | 35% | Ballads work best predictable |
| **Style Influence** | 75% | Holds the Americana/soul sound tightly |

## Step 5 — Generate, then judge
1. Click **Create** — Suno makes 2 versions.
2. Listen for 3 things:
   - **Verses** sound talk-sung, not rushed.
   - **"let it breathe"** echo lands as a backing vocal.
   - **Final chorus** feels bigger than the first two.
3. If a version rushes the long verse lines, drop the BPM to `66 BPM` in the Style box and regenerate.

## Note on the word "Shit" (Verse 3)
The lyrics were labeled "cleaned," but Verse 3 still contains "Shit." For a fully clean version, swap that line to:

```
I keep missin'. Man, I'm good at bein' strong, I'm just tired of bein' by myself.
```
